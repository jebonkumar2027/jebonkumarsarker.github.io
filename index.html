ABOUT ME

Bridging Engineering,
Business & Communication

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate currently working with a Chinese building-material company in Bangladesh as a Chinese Interpreter and Management Assistant.

In my current position, I work closely with the Chinese owner and management and serve as a key communication bridge between Chinese management and the Bangladeshi team.

My responsibilities extend beyond interpretation. I am involved in business, administration, client communication, operational coordination and various activities required to support the company's operations in Bangladesh.

At the same time, I apply my previous experience and knowledge in Mechanical Engineering, technical operations, communication, web development, client handling and business development to solve practical problems and coordinate work across different areas.

Working in a multinational environment has allowed me to combine technical understanding with communication, management and commercial experience. My professional direction continues to develop at the intersection of engineering, technology, business and international collaboration.
